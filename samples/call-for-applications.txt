INDONESIAN INTERNATIONAL STUDENT MOBILITY AWARDS (IISMA) 2026
Call for Applications — Undergraduate Scheme

Applications close 14 September 2026 at 23:59 WIB. Late submissions are not accepted.

ELIGIBILITY
Applicants must be actively enrolled undergraduate students in semesters 4 or 6 at the
time of application, with a minimum GPA of 3.00 out of 4.00, and must not have
previously participated in any government-funded overseas mobility programme.

REQUIRED ESSAYS
All applicants must submit the following. Essays are written in English.

  1. Statement of Motivation — maximum 500 words.
     Explain why you are applying and what you intend to gain.

  2. Contribution Plan — maximum 300 words.
     Describe how you will apply what you learn upon returning to Indonesia.

  3. Personal Statement of Financial Need — no word limit is imposed, but
     applicants are asked to be concise.

  4. Short Bio — maximum 750 characters including spaces.

Essays must be written in formal English, in the first person, and must not be
written on the applicant's behalf by a third party.

SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS
A scanned copy of the applicant's Kartu Tanda Mahasiswa (KTM), an official academic
transcript, and a letter of recommendation from the home faculty must be uploaded
as separate PDF files, each not exceeding 2 MB.
