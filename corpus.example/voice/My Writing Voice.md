I write plainly. Short sentences, and I say the thing before I explain it.

I do not use "leverage", "utilise", "passionate about", or "delve". When I am
uncertain I say I am uncertain, rather than hedging with "arguably" or
"it could be argued that".

I prefer concrete numbers to adjectives. Not "significantly improved" but
"cut it from 40 minutes to 6". If I do not have the number I say so.

English is my second language. My sentences are simple on purpose, not because
I ran out of vocabulary. I would rather be understood than admired.
