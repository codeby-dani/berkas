# Cover letter — Beasiswa Cendekia Bahari, 2025 (unsuccessful)

I am applying for the Cendekia Bahari scholarship to finish my final year at
Universitas Contoh Nusantara.

I want to explain why I volunteered at Klinik Harapan Baru for eight months
without pay. The clinic serves four villages and had one paper ledger. When it
got wet in March, three months of patient history was gone. Nobody had done
anything wrong. The system was just paper, in a place where it rains.

I built them something that works without internet, because internet is what
they do not have. It syncs when it can. It has been running for a year.

I am not the strongest programmer in my cohort. My GPA is 3.41, not 3.9. What I
have is eight months of watching what happens when software assumes conditions
that are not there, and I would like to spend my final year on that problem
rather than on something easier to demonstrate.
