# Past application answers

## "Describe a time you failed." (Cendekia Bahari, 2025 — 150 words)

The first version of the clinic sync assumed the connection would come back
within a day. It did not. Records queued for eleven days, the queue filled the
tablet's storage, and the app stopped accepting new patients. A nurse called me
on a Saturday.

I had tested the offline case. I had not tested the *long* offline case, because
on my laptop the connection always came back. I rewrote it to write straight to
disk and treat sync as optional rather than deferred.

What I took from it: I had tested the condition I could imagine, not the
condition they live in. Now I ask what the worst realistic week looks like
before I write anything.
